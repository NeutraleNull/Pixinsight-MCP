---
title: Sharpen linear data
summary: Deconvolve each channel while it is linear, before the stars are removed.
---

Sharpen before removing stars: the star removal works on the sharpened stars.

Try the settings on a copy of a region first, as `practices/try-settings` describes, in a place with stars and nebula detail.

Afterwards, look at a full-resolution crop around the brightest star. Dots in an arc around it can already be faintly there, and sharpening strengthens them.
