---
title: Separate the stars
summary: Split each channel into a starless image and a star image, so that nebula and stars can be stretched apart.
---

Keep both results of every channel: the starless image goes on to noise reduction and stretching, and the star image to `stages/stars`.

Do not try this on a preview of a plate-solved image. A process that makes a new image from a preview can open a modal error dialog there. Use a copy of a region, as `practices/try-settings` describes.

Beneath dense star clusters, the nebula can come out smeared. The recombined stars usually hide it.
