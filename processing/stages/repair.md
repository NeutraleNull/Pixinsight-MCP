---
title: Repair local defects
summary: Fix what the earlier steps left behind in a few places, such as halos of bright stars, without touching the rest.
---

Repair a defect where it is. A correction of the whole image to hide a local defect costs real signal elsewhere: in one run, limiting the whole sky of OIII and SII to remove halos also removed faint nebulosity.

Skip this stage if there is nothing to repair.
