Reduce noise on starless data, so that the stars do not suffer.

Judge it on a copy of a region that has passed the earlier steps, not on a preview: see `practices/try-settings`. Measure the MAD of an empty sky patch before and after, and look at a full-resolution crop for a wormy or plastic texture.

Later sharpening on a denoised image looked wormy in one run, so sharpen earlier.
