---
title: Frame the image
summary: Choose a crop by composition at the very end, and keep the uncropped image.
---

A crop comes last. It deletes a plate solution that cannot be transformed: the result of `run_process` then says `astrometryRemoved`. So write the uncropped files first, and keep them: they stay the plate-solved version.

Work on a duplicate, and write the cropped files beside the uncropped ones. The user judges the composition on a calibrated screen.
