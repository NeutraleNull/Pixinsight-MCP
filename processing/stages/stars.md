---
title: Bring the stars back
summary: Build star color from the star images, stretch them apart from the nebula, and put them into the image.
---

The star images from `stages/star-separation` are linear and almost empty. Stretch them on their own, more gently than the nebula, so that the nebula leads.

A preview of a star layer uses a stretch of its own, as `practices/look` describes. Judge the result in full-resolution crops of the stretched star image, under `stretch: "none"`. They should be small and round, with colors from orange to blue-white.

Spikes of the brightest stars can show blue and orange apart, because they differ between the channels.
