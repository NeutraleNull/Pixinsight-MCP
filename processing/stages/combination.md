---
title: Combine the channels into color
summary: Turn the stretched starless channels into one color image, with a palette chosen together with the user.
---

Every palette for narrowband data is a design choice. Decide how much design the user wants, and say which method you used.

- **Close to the channels:** the colors still show which emission is where.
- **Designed:** brightness and color are built apart, which gives more depth and a wider range of colors, but the image no longer shows a direct assignment of channel to color.

If the user has not said, ask, or make both and show them side by side under `stretch: "none"`.

Afterwards measure the hue of warm and of blue regions, the saturation where they meet, and the sky, as `practices/measure` describes.
