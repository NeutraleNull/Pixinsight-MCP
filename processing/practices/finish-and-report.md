---
title: Finish and report honestly
summary: What to check before calling an image finished, and what to tell the user about it.
---

## Before calling a result finished

- Check the sky in several places, the darkest nebula areas, and the brightest nebula areas at full resolution.
- Check dark clouds, star clusters, and the area around the brightest star.
- Compare the final export with the working image. A preview of a large image can hide compression.

## Report

- **What you assumed,** such as a filter bandwidth, or a share that you estimated.
- **What you could not check.** The user sees the image on a calibrated screen, and you do not.
- **Where the image is designed rather than measured.** A palette, a crop and added depth are design choices.
- **Where it is worse than another version,** not only where it is better.
